# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential employers, clients, and collaborators browsing the portfolio. They're skimming — they want to understand who this is and what they've made, quickly. The site itself is part of the work: its quality signals the quality of everything in it.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio website that showcases work with Awwwards-level craft. Everything lives on one page — sections for work, about, and contact, navigated by scroll or anchor. Design-first but minimal: easy to scan, pleasing in the eyes. The site demonstrates taste, execution, and attention to detail. It is itself a portfolio piece.

## Brand Personality

Typography-forward, crafted, restrained. The personality comes through type choices, layout rhythm, and precise detail — not through color overload or decorative elements. Confident without shouting. Designer without feeling overdesigned.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS defaults**: blue-purple gradients, white backgrounds, rounded-card grids, generic illustration style, the startup-template look
- **Brutalist/terminal aesthetic**: monospace-heavy, raw-HTML, intentionally-ugly — this should feel designed, not raw
- **Maximalist chaos**: overloaded layouts, competing elements, visual noise for its own sake

## Design Principles

1. **Typography carries the design.** Type choice, scale, and spacing do the heavy lifting. Hierarchy is clear at a glance.

2. **Confident restraint.** Minimal not because it's bare, but because every element earns its place. Nothing decorative survives scrutiny.

3. **Craft is the signal.** Precise spacing, thoughtful motion, intentional color. The site's quality signals the work's quality — it is part of the portfolio.

4. **Scan-first, not sparse.** Content is easy to consume at a glance but never feels empty. Breathing room is intentional, not absence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Sufficient color contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader support. Motion should be present and purposeful but respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
